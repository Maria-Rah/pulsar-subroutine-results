# Captions of the 20 individual tracking diagrams (Run 4)

Two diagrams per neutron star. **FigA**: four panels, (i) P–Ṗ with the 10⁹ G dipole locus, (ii) P–B with the 10⁹ G level, (iii) Ṗ–B with dipole lines for three representative periods, (iv) B against the characteristic age τ = P/2Ṗ. **FigB**: scenario bar and the time series of P, Ṗ, B, E_total, and E_bind, with dashed lines at the label changes.

Symbols: fill colour identifies the neutron star; ring colour gives the label at each record (gold S1, black S5, navy S7); START and END mark the first and last records. Background shading follows the label. Only records with Ṗ > 0 can appear on the logarithmic axes of FigA. Compared with the original diagrams, only the S7 legend entry (now “S7: NS–BH binary”, the reference name of the paper) and the energy axis labels (now “code units”) were changed; every other pixel is the original.

The recorded values are those written by the module version of Run 4. In that version P and B are drawn at the first call, P and Ṗ do not change between most outputs, the spin state follows the particle index, and the S5/S7 labels follow the particle taken from the neighbour list (paper, Sect. 5.2). The diagrams therefore document the record, not a physical spin evolution.
